PROMPT 2 POSTER

Everything participants need to know — rules, rounds, judging criteria and awards for the poster-generation challenge.

01 — REGULATIONS

General Rules & Regulations

No External Electronic Devices
Mobile phones, smartwatches, tablets, USB drives, or secondary laptops are strictly prohibited inside the competition arena.
Attendance & Registration
All participants must report to their assigned workstations 15 minutes before the start time for attendance verification.
Fair Play
Any form of communication between participants, prompt-copying, or unauthorized internet browsing results in immediate disqualification.

02 — FORMAT

Competition Format & Rounds

01 STAGE 1

Preliminary Generation & Qualification

WRITTEN — 5 MIN

Write your detailed image prompt on paper based on the provided theme.

TYPING & RENDERING — 5 MIN

Input your written prompt into the designated AI poster generator to render your entry.

Qualifying: top-scoring entries advance based on visual appeal, theme alignment and composition.
02 STAGE 2

Final Advanced Challenge

WRITTEN REFINEMENT — 5 MIN

Plan a multi-concept prompt scenario on paper.

TYPING & RENDERING — 5 MIN

Generate your final entry in the rendering engine.

For selected participants only.

03 — EVALUATION

Submission & Judging Criteria

01

Relevance to Theme

How accurately the generated poster reflects the given competition topic.

02

Creativity & Originality

Unique artistic direction, creative prompt styling, and original concept execution.

03

Visual Quality & Composition

Balance of visual elements, color harmony, text legibility, and rendering precision.

04

Prompt Efficiency

How effectively the written text guides the AI engine without parameter clutter.
